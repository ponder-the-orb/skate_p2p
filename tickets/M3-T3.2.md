SPEC: ROADMAP.md M3 ("attempt countdown timer"); ARCHITECTURE.md §5
trust model; ADR-003 (facts on the wire, never conclusions — which is
exactly why this timer must not act).
NOTES (Architect rulings):
  - ADVISORY ONLY. At 0:00 the display changes ("TIME — land it or
    bail") and nothing else happens. No auto-bail, no event, no
    packet: a timeout is a conclusion, and we don't put conclusions
    on the wire or in the engine. Enforced variant → parking lot.
  - Shown ONLY to the attempting player: setter in state 2, defender
    in state 4. The waiting player's screen is unchanged — two clocks
    can't be trusted to agree; one clock can't lie to its owner.
  - 60 seconds: `const attemptSeconds = 60` beside the widget.
    Restarts whenever its state is (re)entered: new trick declared,
    each new defense.
  - attempt_timer.dart is a self-contained StatefulWidget owning its
    Timer; MUST cancel in dispose() — leaked timers fail widget tests
    on their own, let that guard you. Match the dark panel styling.
  - No new dependencies; Timer from dart:async only.
ACCEPTANCE:
  - attempt_timer_test: renders full time; counts down under
    tester.pump; flips to the time's-up treatment at zero; disposes
    cleanly (no pending-timer failures).
  - match_screen_test: timer present in states 2 and 4, absent in
    1/3/5/6; declaring a new trick restarts it.
  - flutter analyze clean · flutter test green · format check passes
  - CHANGELOG under [Unreleased] · five-line report + PR link
OUT OF SCOPE: enforcement/auto-bail, settings, sounds, any diff to
engine/net/state/server/protocol (must be zero).
IF IT BALLOONS: it shouldn't — stop and report if it does.
